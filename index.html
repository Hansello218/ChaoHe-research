Computer Vision & Computer Graphics

Chao He

I graduated from Soochow University. My research interests lie in computer vision and computer graphics, with a particular focus on 3D/4D reconstruction, Gaussian Splatting, physics-based simulation, and surgical robotics.

I am interested in developing geometry-aware and physics-informed methods for reconstructing, understanding, and simulating dynamic 3D environments.
